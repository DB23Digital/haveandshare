What Is PQ Intelligence?

PQ (Positivity Quotient) measures the relative strength of your positive mental muscles versus your negative ones – your "Saboteurs". PQ Intelligence is your capacity to respond to life and work challenges with a positive, resourceful mindset instead of a negative, sabotaging one.

The Positive Intelligence® program, created by Shirzad Chamine, is a research-based mental fitness framework that combines neuroscience, positive psychology, performance science, and coaching. It is delivered via a powerful app, weekly group coaching, and daily "PQ reps" that rewire the brain for more Sage and less Saboteur.

Raising PQ has been shown to systematically improve core emotional intelligence (EQ) competencies, including self-awareness, empathy, conflict management, adaptability, and collaboration. In just six to eight weeks, many organisations see measurable shifts in engagement and leadership behaviour when PQ is embedded properly.

Teams that strengthen their PQ typically report:

  • Higher performance and productivity

  • Better decision-making under pressure
  • Stronger relationships and trust
  • Reduced stress, anxiety and burnout

How The PQ Intelligence Program Works

Have & Share offers a structured PQ Intelligence journey that blends the official PQ app with expert coaching and corporate facilitation.

Frequently Asked Questions About PQ® Coaching in South Africa

What is Positive Intelligence (PQ®)?

Positive Intelligence (PQ®) is a science-backed mental fitness framework developed by Shirzad Chamine that measures the strength of your positive mental muscles versus your inner Saboteurs. A higher PQ score means you respond to challenges with clarity and creativity rather than stress, anxiety, or self-sabotage.

What is a PQ score and how is it measured?

Your PQ score (Positivity Quotient) is measured on a scale of 0 to 100 and reflects the percentage of time your mind acts as your ally rather than your enemy. A score above 75 is the tipping point at which mental fitness enables peak performance. You take the free Saboteur Assessment and PQ score test on the Positive Intelligence website.

Is there a PQ® coach in South Africa?

Yes. Claudina Hafenscher is a PQ® Certified Coach based in Johannesburg, South Africa, and is listed on the official Positive Intelligence Coach Marketplace. She delivers the full PQ Intelligence program to individuals, executives, and corporate teams in Gauteng and online across South Africa.

How long does the PQ Intelligence program take?

The core Positive Intelligence mental fitness program runs for 6 to 8 weeks, requiring just 10 to 15 minutes of daily practice through the PQ app. Many participants experience measurable shifts in mental fitness, emotional regulation, and leadership performance within the first three weeks.

What is the difference between PQ and EQ (Emotional Intelligence)?

EQ (Emotional Intelligence) describes your ability to recognise and manage emotions. PQ (Positive Intelligence) is a trainable mental fitness practice that actively strengthens your capacity to act on that awareness - converting good intentions into consistent, wise behaviour under pressure. PQ training raises EQ in practice, not just theory.
