What is mBIT (Multiple Brain Integration Techniques) Coaching?

mBIT is a neuroscience-based coaching methodology that works with the "three brains" physically present in the human body. Because decisions at the executive level are rarely clear-cut, leaders are forced to make high-impact choices when data is incomplete and stakeholders are diverse. When everything is shifting, over-reliance on one specific "brain" shows up inevitably as over-thinking, freezing entirely, or pushing too hard and alienating people.

The Head Brain

Cognition: thinking, analysing, planning

The Heart Brain

Values: connection, empathy, meaning

The Gut Brain

Risk: courage, instinct, action

mBIT coaching fundamentally helps leaders notice exactly which intelligence is overriding their behaviour in critical moments. By actively integrating all three, they can respond with far greater wisdom rather than defaulting to automatic, reactive patterns.

Why mBIT Matters for C-Suite Leaders

At senior levels, the cost of a wrong move can be enormous. Logic alone cannot solve complex human ecosystems. mBIT addresses the core realities of executive execution.

Strategic decisions under pressure

Leaders learn to combine rigorous analysis (head) with values and impact (heart) and risk appetite/decisiveness (gut), leading to more balanced, sustainable choices in ambiguous conditions.

Leading through change and uncertainty

When the context is volatile, mBIT builds the active capacity to stay completely centred and congruent, preventing the common reactions of freezing or aggressively overreacting.

Complex stakeholder relationships

Heart-brain integration supports intense empathy and trust-building, while the head and gut intelligently keep actual conversations grounded in clear facts, firm boundaries, and accountability.

Personal resilience and wellbeing

By recognising the early somatic physical signals of extreme stress or misalignment, executives can quickly adjust how they work and lead, tangibly reducing the emotional cost of their senior roles.

Frequently Asked Questions About mBIT Coaching in South Africa

What is mBIT coaching?

mBIT (Multiple Brain Integration Techniques) is a neuroscience-based coaching methodology that works with the three intelligence centres (or 'brains') in the human body: the head (cognition), the heart (values and empathy), and the gut (courage and instinct). mBIT coaching helps leaders align all three for wiser, more congruent decisions.

Is there an mBIT coach in South Africa?

Yes. Claudina Hafenscher is a Master mBIT Coach based in Johannesburg, South Africa. She is one of very few Master mBIT Coaches in South Africa and delivers individual and executive mBIT coaching sessions in-person in Gauteng and online across South Africa and internationally.

What is the difference between mBIT and mBraining?

mBraining is the broader concept of having multiple intelligences in the body. mBIT (Multiple Brain Integration Techniques) is the formal, structured coaching methodology built on mBraining research. mBIT coaching uses specific techniques to actively integrate the head, heart, and gut intelligences for better leadership outcomes.

Who benefits most from mBIT coaching?

mBIT coaching is most powerful for executives, C-suite leaders, and high-performing individuals who face complex, high-stakes decisions and find they are over-thinking, avoiding conflict, or leading from a reactive rather than grounded state. It is also highly effective for individuals navigating major life transitions.

How many mBIT coaching sessions do I need?

A foundational mBIT coaching engagement typically involves 4 to 8 sessions over 2 to 4 months. Many clients notice a meaningful shift in their decision-making clarity and emotional regulation within the first 2 to 3 sessions.
