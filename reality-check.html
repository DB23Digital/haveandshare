The Core of Reality Check

Around the world – and particularly in South Africa's volatile market – CEOs and executive teams are under pressure to deliver growth while managing rising people, culture, and reputation risks. Organisational health is no longer a "soft" issue; it is a leading indicator of execution, resilience, and financial performance. The Reality Check™ EcoMetric gives C-suite leaders a rigorous, evidence-based way to see how well their organisation is really functioning, long before problems show up in the P&L.

Instead of relying on anecdote or engagement scores that don't move the needle, Reality Check™ translates employee experience and behaviour into a Human Capital Balanced Scorecard and Well-Being Indicator (WBI™). These metrics sit alongside revenue, margin, and operational KPIs, enabling executives and boards to make confident decisions about strategy, structure, and investment in people.

For South African organisations, it offers a globally relevant methodology that is sensitive to local realities – and scalable across regions as the business grows.

As an accredited Reality Check™ coach, Have & Share works directly with executive teams to interpret these insights, align them with strategic priorities, and turn data into clear, board-ready action plans.

What CEOs and Boards Gain From Reality Check™

For C-suite leaders and boards, Reality Check™ is first and foremost a governance, risk, and performance tool. It provides a defensible, repeatable way to measure organisational health – and to link it explicitly to business outcomes. That matters when you are accountable to shareholders, regulators, and talent markets that increasingly scrutinise culture and people risk.

Early visibility of systemic risk

Reality Check™ surfaces issues such as burnout, conflict, silos, weak leadership behaviours, and low trust before they crystallise into lost revenue, customer churn, or reputational damage. Executives gain an "early warning radar" across regions, functions, and levels.

Better capital allocation and ROI on people investments

By quantifying where health is strong or fragile, the EcoMetric helps the C-suite target investment – leadership programmes, restructuring, technology, or workforce initiatives – where it will create the most value. This enables a clearer ROI story for spend on culture, change, and wellbeing.

A clear narrative for boards and integrated reports

The Human Capital Balanced Scorecard and Well-Being Indicator provide coherent metrics and a storyline executives can take to the board: what the health of the organisation is, how it affects strategy execution, and what is being done about it. This strengthens governance and satisfies growing expectations around human capital disclosure.

Scalable insight for global leadership

For organisations expanding from South Africa into other markets, Reality Check™ offers a consistent framework that can be applied across countries and business units. The C-suite can compare health profiles across geographies, identify patterns, and ensure that culture and strategy stay aligned as the organisation grows.

Translating Insight into Strategic Execution

With an accredited partner like Have & Share, C-suite leaders do not just receive a report – they gain an experienced thinking partner who can help translate insight into strategic decisions, leveraging mBIT and PQ Intelligence for concrete execution plans.
