Frequently Asked Questions

What is life coaching?

Life coaching is a transformational process that addresses the whole person (mind, body, and emotions). Unlike traditional coaching, it integrates techniques like PQ® mental fitness, mBIT (Multiple Brain Integration), and Educational Kinesiology to uncover and resolve the root causes of self-sabotage, not just surface symptoms.

How long is a life coaching session in South Africa?

Each individual life coaching session with Claudina Hafenscher in South Africa typically lasts 60 to 90 minutes. Sessions are available in-person in Johannesburg, Gauteng, or online for clients across South Africa and internationally.

What methodologies does Claudina Hafenscher use in coaching?

Claudina uses a unique blend of Positive Intelligence (PQ®) for mental fitness, mBIT coaching to align head, heart, and gut intelligence, Educational Kinesiology to restore cognitive focus, and the Reality Check™ EcoMetric tool for data-driven personal and organisational health insights.

How many coaching sessions do I need?

Most clients experience significant breakthroughs within 3 to 6 sessions. A full coaching journey typically spans 3 to 6 months depending on your goals, with sessions held fortnightly. Claudina begins with a complimentary discovery session to assess fit before committing to a programme.

Is online life coaching as effective as in-person?

Yes. Claudina Hafenscher delivers highly effective coaching sessions online via video call for clients across South Africa and internationally. The depth of transformation is comparable to in-person sessions, and online coaching removes all geographic barriers for clients outside Johannesburg.

What is the difference between life coaching and therapy?

Life coaching focuses on future goals and unlocking potential, while therapy typically addresses diagnosed mental health conditions and past trauma. Life coaching is action-oriented and growth-focused. Claudina is a COMENSA Accredited Senior Coach, not a therapist, and her work complements but does not replace professional mental health treatment.
