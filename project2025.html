What is Project 2025?

Project 2025 is a national-scale initiative that addresses the guidance gap in global schools and builds an employability portfolio for every learner, so students make informed subject choices, discover pathways, and connect to real opportunities. Have & Share operationalises Project 2025 with SkillsPassport's guidance platform, turning corporate sponsorship into measurable talent pipelines and real outcomes.

Low-anxiety, story-led diagnostics across verbal, numerical and non-verbal reasoning with personality insights.

Clear, evidence-based subject recommendations aligned to targeted careers and TVET/university pathways.

Career days, job shadowing and projects with corporate partners to build meaningful artifacts and confidence.

The Have & Share + SkillsPassport Solution

  • End-to-end delivery partner: programme design, school onboarding, educator enablement, and on-the-ground coordination.
  • SkillsPassport platform: assessments, guidance engine, learner portfolio, analytics dashboards for schools & sponsors.
  • Integrated Education Impact: transparent impact tracking tied to talent development and regional pipelines.
  • Safeguards: POPIA-aware data handling, opt-in consent, and equitable access principles.

Corporate Value & ROI

Align sponsorship with workforce planning. Convert community investment into pipeline readiness and recruitment efficiency, with transparent metrics you can report to boards and stakeholders. Access cohorts with verified reasoning, personality fit, and practical artifacts. Shortlist faster using standardized data and portfolios; reduce mis-hire risk.

Implementation & Management

Claudina Hafenscher and Have & Share manage the full lifecycle of Project 2025 delivery, from stakeholder alignment and school coordination to day-to-day execution, impact tracking, and sponsor reporting. Roadmap: The First 1,000 Days

  1. Define target schools, grades, and cohort sizes with sponsors.
  2. Schedule assessments & guidance sessions with educator support.
  3. Run exposure events and portfolio-building projects with partners.
  4. Report outcomes and refine next-cohort targets.

What Sponsors Can Expect to Measure

Project 2025 is designed for measurable education and employability impact. Sponsors can track the number of learners assessed, the schools reached, subject-choice recommendations delivered, career pathways explored, and portfolio artifacts created. The aim is to turn sponsorship into a visible talent-development pipeline rather than a once-off donation.

  • Learner readiness: each participant receives clearer insight into subject choices, strengths, and possible career paths.
  • School support: educators and guidance teams receive structured information to support better learner decisions.
  • Corporate link: sponsors can connect career exposure, job shadowing, and future skills conversations to real local industries.

How to Join the Programme

Parents can purchase an individual assessment for a learner. Sponsors can fund a school cohort, a regional pilot, or a themed pathway connected to scarce skills, youth employability, or community development.
