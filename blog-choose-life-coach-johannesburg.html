How to Choose a Life Coach in Johannesburg or Online

Choosing a coach is personal. The right decision should feel safe, practical, and grounded in visible evidence.

How do you choose a life coach in Johannesburg or online?

Choose a life coach by checking visible credentials, coaching approach, boundaries, testimonials or real proof, and whether the first conversation tests fit without pressure. If you are in Johannesburg, ask whether in-person work is practical. If you are outside Gauteng, confirm that online coaching is structured and private.

Questions to ask before you book

A good discovery process lowers anxiety. It gives you space to ask direct questions about credentials, confidentiality, methods, and whether coaching is the right support route.

  • Ask what training, accreditation, and experience the coach brings to your specific issue.
  • Ask how online sessions work, how progress is reviewed, and what happens if coaching is not the best fit.
  • For South African context, check local availability, remote options, and whether the coach understands your personal or workplace environment.
