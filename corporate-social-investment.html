Transforming Corporate Social Investment: The Have & Share Strategy. Bridging the gap between corporate profitability and sustainable social impact.

From Transactional Charity to Transformational Change

In the evolving landscape of global business, Corporate Social Investment (CSI) has shifted from a "nice-to-have" philanthropic gesture to a critical business imperative. Claudina Hafenscher is leading this charge by extending the Have & Share brand into the CSI space. Many companies view CSI as a compliance requirement. However, Have & Share advocates for a transformational approach. Instead of one-off donations, we focus on strategic investments that create measurable, long-term shifts in society.

Key Pillars of the Have & Share CSI Framework:

  • Strategic Alignment:

    Integrating social responsibility into the core business strategy to ensure sustainability.

  • Empowering Marginalized Communities:

    Prioritizing high-impact projects that support youth, women, and people with disabilities.

  • Capacity Building for NPOs:

    We move beyond funding projects to funding the people behind them, ensuring Non-Profit Organizations have the administrative strength to succeed.
