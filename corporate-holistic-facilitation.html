Frequently Asked Questions

What is corporate facilitation?

Corporate facilitation is an immersive coaching programme that breaks down organisational silos, improves cross-departmental communication, and transforms unproductive meetings into collaborative innovation sessions. It integrates mBIT intelligence techniques, Educational Kinesiology, and mindfulness-based leadership tools.

Is corporate facilitation available in Johannesburg and across South Africa?

Yes. Claudina Hafenscher is based in Glen Marais, Gauteng, and delivers corporate facilitation in-person across Johannesburg, Pretoria, and other South African cities. Remote and international facilitation via video conferencing is also available.

What size organisations do you work with?

Claudina works with a diverse range of organisations, from smaller enterprise teams to large multinationals. She has successfully facilitated sessions for over 10,000 delegates across 15 major corporations in South Africa and internationally.

What measurable results can we expect from corporate facilitation?

Organisations typically report improved cross-departmental collaboration, reduced meeting time and conflict, stronger psychological safety, and better decision-making alignment within 3 to 6 months. The Reality Checkâ„¢ EcoMetric tool can quantify culture and engagement shifts over time.

How is corporate facilitation different from standard corporate training?

Standard corporate training delivers information. Corporate facilitation creates transformation. It works on the underlying human dynamics (values alignment, psychological safety, and the somatic and emotional patterns that drive team behaviour) rather than just teaching new frameworks or skills.
