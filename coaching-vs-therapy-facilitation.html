Choosing Support

Coaching vs Therapy vs Mentoring vs Facilitation

Not every kind of support does the same job. This guide helps individuals and leaders choose the right route with less anxiety.

What is the difference between coaching, therapy, mentoring, and facilitation?

Coaching is a forward-looking partnership that helps you build awareness, make decisions, and practise new behaviour. Therapy is clinical support for mental health diagnosis, trauma, or deeper psychological treatment. Mentoring shares experience and guidance from someone further along a path. Facilitation helps a group have better conversations, make decisions, and turn agreements into action.

RouteBest forMain focus
CoachingGrowth, decisions, confidence, actionForward movement and accountability
TherapyClinical or deeper psychological supportHealing and treatment
MentoringLearning from experienceGuidance and perspective
FacilitationTeams and leadership groupsShared clarity and better group decisions

When each option fits

The right route depends on the problem, risk level, and desired outcome. A discovery conversation can help clarify whether coaching or facilitation is appropriate, and a responsible coach should recommend therapy when clinical support is the safer route.

  • Coaching fits when you want clarity, accountability, mental fitness, or aligned action.
  • Therapy may be more appropriate for trauma, clinical mental health concerns, or crisis support.
  • Corporate facilitation fits when the issue lives in the team system, not only in one individual.
