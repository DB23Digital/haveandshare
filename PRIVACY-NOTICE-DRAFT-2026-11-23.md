# Have & Share privacy notice: draft for Claudina's review

Drafted 23/11/2026. Not live. Replaces `privacy-policy.html` (last updated 1 June 2026) once Claudina approves it.

This is a working draft, not legal advice. Before it goes live, a data-protection adviser should check the UK and EU sections, in particular whether Have & Share needs a UK or EU representative.

Items marked **[CONFIRM]** are things we don't know about how Have & Share handles data. Please answer each one or correct the wording. The draft should describe what you actually do, not what a template says.

## Questions for Claudina first

1. **Enquiries.** Do enquiries arrive only by email, WhatsApp, phone and the booking page (book.haveandshare.net)? The current policy mentions Google Forms and a mailing list. Do you still use either?
2. **Booking system.** The current policy names "VanX" for bookings. Is that still the right name for the system behind book.haveandshare.net? The site also loads a tracking script from that system on every page.
3. **Coaching notes.** Where do you keep session notes (paper, a notebook app, Google Drive, something else)? Who else can see them? The current policy says you keep them for at least 5 years after an engagement ends. Is that right, and why 5 years (COMENSA guidance, insurance, other)?
4. **Team diagnostics.** For Reality Check® using EcoMetric™ and similar team assessments: who runs the survey platform, where is the data stored, do you see individual answers or only team results, and how long is it kept? Who at the client company receives the report?
5. **Children.** The current policy says your services are not for anyone under 18, but Educational Kinesiology and Brain Gym® work, and the My Future Today simulation, include children and learners. Do you work with under-18s now? If so, the notice needs a section on parental consent.
6. **Newsletter.** Do you send a newsletter or marketing emails? If so, through which service?
7. **Cookie banner.** The site runs Google Analytics without asking visitors first. UK and EU rules generally need consent before analytics cookies are set. Are you happy for us to add a cookie consent banner? (Deon's call on the build.)
