## 2. The situation, in plain terms

Have & Share has four blog pages about imposter syndrome. All four are well written. That is not the problem.

The problem is that they are all writing about the same thing. All four explain what the Judge Saboteur is. Three explain the Hyper-Achiever. Three explain the mBIT three-brain model. Three quote the same six to eight week timeline. Two open with functionally the same question answered with the same sentence stem. Two carry title tags targeting the same commercial phrase.

When four pages on one site compete for one search term, Google cannot tell which one is the answer, so it tends to rank none of them well. Imposter syndrome is Have & Share's single largest source of search demand, and the cluster currently underperforms because of this.

The fix is not to delete pages. All four target genuinely different questions and all four deserve to exist. The fix is to make each page unmistakably about one thing.

## 5. Brand voice standard

Taken from the Have & Share Brand Bible, version 1.0. This is the client's own standard, not a preference of mine.

### The four character traits, all present simultaneously

**Empathetic.** Speak to the real human challenge beneath the professional one. Acknowledge the struggle before offering the solution.

**Authoritative.** Reference proven frameworks. Cite methodology. Demonstrate expertise without arrogance.

**Transformative.** Language centres on movement. From stuck to clear, from potential to performance.

**Grounded.** Warm, not gushing. Professional, not cold. Avoid corporate jargon and wellness clichés in equal measure.

### The five voice principles

1. **Second person always.** "You", never "clients", "one", or "participants".
2. **Empathy before expertise.** Lead with the challenge, not the solution.
3. **Authority, not complexity.** Use the language of science, not jargon. Reference PQ® and neuroscience, but always explain them plainly.
4. **Precision over platitude.** "Reduce your threat response in under 90 seconds" beats "improve your wellbeing".
5. **Respect, not rescue.** We are a partner, not a saviour. We illuminate. The reader decides.

### If the brand were a person

She is the most capable person in the room, but you would never know it from how she enters. She listens before she speaks. She carries fifteen years of experience lightly, as earned wisdom rather than status. She is the coach who tells you the truth in a way that makes you want to hear it.

Every sentence you write should sound like her.

### Banned vocabulary

| Use | Never use |
|---|---|
| Mental fitness | Hustle |
| Alignment | Life-changing |
| Performance | Breakthrough |
| Clarity | Journey (used alone) |
| Potential | Mindset hack |
| Evidence-based | Holistic solutions |
| Transformation | World-class |
| Grounded | Unlock your potential |

Also avoid, as general AI and marketing tells: delve, tapestry, landscape (figurative), navigate (figurative), elevate, empower, game-changer, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y", and any sentence that opens with "Imagine".

## 6. Mechanical rules, non-negotiable

**No em dashes. Anywhere. Ever.** This is a hard house rule across all Have & Share content. Use commas, colons, full stops or brackets. Note that the Brand Bible itself contains em dashes because it predates the rule. The rule wins.

**South African English throughout.** Organisation, realise, practise (verb) and practice (noun), behaviour, programme, centre, judgement. Set your spellchecker to en-ZA before you start.

**Trademark symbols.** PQ® and Positive Intelligence® carry the registered symbol on first use per page, then plain thereafter. Reality Check™ carries the trademark symbol on first use per page. Note that a previous encoding bug on this site rendered these symbols as corrupted characters. Check them in the rendered page, not just in your draft.

**Second person, active voice.** No passive constructions where an active one exists.

**Sentence and paragraph length.** Vary sentence length deliberately. Paragraphs of one to four sentences. No paragraph longer than five lines on mobile.

**No exclamation marks.** Not one.

**Heading case.** Sentence case for H3 and below. Title case for H1 and H2, matching the pack exactly.

**No bold for emphasis inside body prose** beyond what the pack specifies. Bold is used in the pack to label list items, not to shout.

## 7. SEO mechanics you own

### Title tags: all four in the pack need trimming

I specified titles in the pack that run too long. Google truncates at roughly 60 characters. Verified counts:

| Page | Pack version | Chars | Use this instead | Chars |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hub | How to Overcome Imposter Syndrome at Work: A Practical Guide \| Have & Share | 75 | How to Overcome Imposter Syndrome at Work \| Have & Share | 56 |
| High performer | Why Success Doesn't Cure Imposter Syndrome for High Performers \| Have & Share | 77 | Why Success Doesn't Cure Imposter Syndrome \| Have & Share | 57 |
| Executive | Executive Self-Doubt: Why Capable Leaders Doubt Themselves Most \| Have & Share | 78 | Executive Self-Doubt: Why Capable Leaders Doubt Themselves | 58 |
| Coaching | Imposter Syndrome Coaching: What Actually Happens in Sessions \| Have & Share | 76 | Imposter Syndrome Coaching: What to Expect \| Have & Share | 57 |

Rule for any future title: keep the unique portion under 45 characters so the " | Have & Share" suffix fits. Where the unique portion needs the room, drop the brand suffix, as on the executive page above.

The H1 does not need to match the title tag and should stay as written in the pack. The longer H1 is better on the page. The shorter title tag is better in results.

### Everything else

**Meta descriptions:** 140 to 160 characters. Write to earn the click, not to repeat the title. Each must be unique across the four pages.

**One H1 per page.** The H1 in the pack, exactly.

**Answer box format:** every page opens with an H2 phrased as a question, followed by a 40 to 60 word direct answer. The four questions must be genuinely different questions. This is the format most likely to be pulled into an AI Overview, and it is also where two of the four pages currently duplicate each other. Check all four side by side before you sign off.

**FAQ schema:** three to five questions per page, all rendered visibly on the page, and no question repeated on any other page in the cluster. Schema only ever describes content that is actually on the page.

**Internal links:** exactly as specified in the pack. Each spoke links up to the hub once with the anchor `how to overcome imposter syndrome`, and across to the coaching page once with the anchor `imposter syndrome coaching`. No spoke links to another spoke. Do not add helpful extra links.

**No keyword stuffing.** The target term should appear in the title, the H1, the answer box, and naturally two or three times in the body. That is sufficient. If a sentence reads oddly because a keyword has been forced into it, the keyword loses.

**Image alt text:** descriptive of the image content, not the keyword. The existing hero alt text on all four pages is good. Keep it.

## 8. Accuracy and claims policy

This is a coaching practice writing about a mental health adjacent topic. The standard is higher than for general marketing content.

**No invented statistics.** If a number is not in the pack, do not add one. If you find one you think is useful, send Deon the primary source and let him decide.

**Two specific claims have already been corrected.** They are documented in section 7 of the pack. Do not restore the originals from the live pages.

- The six to eight week timeline is attributed to Positive Intelligence's own programme data, not to independent research. Keep that attribution.
- The "seven in ten" Harvard Business Review statistic has been removed entirely. Do not reinstate it.

**No therapeutic or medical claims.** Coaching is not treatment. Imposter syndrome is not a diagnosis, and the hub says so explicitly. Nothing may imply that coaching treats anxiety, depression or any clinical condition.

**Keep the therapy boundary visible.** The coaching page states that coaching is not a substitute for therapy and that Claudina will say so where therapy is the more appropriate route. That line stays, in full. It is both ethically correct and commercially credible.

**No guarantees.** The pack explicitly says coaching does not remove the Saboteurs and that any programme promising that is overselling. Keep that. It is one of the strongest trust signals on the page.

**Credentials:** COMENSA Accredited Senior Coach and Certified PQ® Coach. Exactly that wording. Do not embellish.

## 10. Tone calibration per page

The four pages meet the reader in four different states. The voice is the same. The temperature is not.

**Hub.** Explanatory and steady. The reader is orienting themselves and may be encountering the concept properly for the first time. Warm, but the job here is clarity.

**High performer.** Recognition first. This reader is quietly frustrated and has been told "you'll feel it when you achieve X" three times too often. The page earns trust in the first paragraph by describing their experience more precisely than they expected. Slightly wry is fine. Sympathetic is not.

**Executive.** The most reserved of the four. This reader is senior, private, and unlikely to have said any of this out loud. Do not be familiar with them. No reassurance, no encouragement, no "you've got this". Respect and precision only. The unspoken promise of the page is discretion.

**Coaching.** Practical and unhurried. The reader is close to booking and is looking for reasons not to. Answer the objection before they finish forming it. Never sell. The page converts by being specific, not by being persuasive.

## 14. What success looks like

Thirty to sixty days after deployment, for each of the three head terms in the cluster, one Have & Share URL should be rising while the others hold steady or drop away from that term.

The failure signal is two URLs from this site continuing to trade positions on the same query. That means the pages are still competing, which would mean a concept has crept back onto a page that does not own it.

That is the entire job. Four pages, four questions, no overlap.
