Common Questions

Coaching Questions for Johannesburg and Online Clients

A few practical answers before you book a discovery conversation.

How do you choose a coach in South Africa?

A good coach in South Africa should have visible credentials, a clear method, real client proof, and a discovery process that checks fit before commitment. For personal growth, look for psychological safety and practical accountability. For corporate teams, look for facilitation experience and measurable workplace outcomes.

Check Claudina's credentials Start with a discovery conversation
What is the difference between coaching and therapy?

Coaching is a forward-looking partnership that helps you build awareness, clarify goals, strengthen mental fitness, and take aligned action. Therapy is clinical support for mental health diagnosis, trauma treatment, and deeper psychological healing. If therapy is the better route, I will say so.

Do you offer in-person and online coaching options?

Yes. Sessions are available in person in the Johannesburg and Gauteng area where practical, and online for clients across South Africa and globally. Online coaching is structured, private, and effective for both individuals and leadership teams.

Can I work with a life coach if I am based outside Johannesburg?

Yes. Have & Share Coaching supports clients across South Africa and internationally through online sessions. Johannesburg-based clients can discuss in-person options during the discovery conversation.

What happens in the free discovery session?

The discovery session is a calm, practical conversation about what you want support with, what feels stuck, and whether the coaching approach is a good fit. There is no pressure to commit.
